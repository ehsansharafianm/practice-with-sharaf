Practice with Sharaf

Practise English or Spanish, one skill at a time. Choose your language:

✍️

Dictation Practice

Listen to a word and write it. Learn new words, review old ones, and track your daily score. Spanish letters (á, ñ, ¿…) are one click away.
🗣️

Pronunciation Practice

See the phonetics (IPA), syllables and stress of any word, hear a woman's and a man's voice, and practise saying it.
🔁

Shadowing Practice

Paste a YouTube link in English or Spanish and shadow the real speaker, sentence by sentence. Or use your own sentences and texts. Check your words, rhythm and melody (intonation).
📚

Vocabulary

Build your own word library: type or say a word, see its meaning and how to say it, then learn it with flashcards and a quiz.
